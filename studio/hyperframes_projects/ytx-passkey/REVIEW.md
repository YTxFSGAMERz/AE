## Creative calls

The film is a defensive explainer a viewer can search for: a passkey cannot be typed into a fake page, and a phone call can still put someone else's key on the account. Three checks, on a Windows PC the viewer opens themselves. No attack steps, no domains, no recovery phrase, no commands.

Picture is drawn in code: paper, ink, one amber shape, a hard print shadow, a 4 px rule that draws across the whole 351.58 s so a hold is never a frozen frame. HyperFrames is the finish. After Effects 2026 was launched twice and never ran the cold-open script, so the film was not rendered twice and After Effects was not retried.

The read is local Chatterbox on the RTX 3050. It is still a synthesizer. `assets/narration.txt` is the script to record over. The bed is a written D-minor motif (`assets/music/motif.wav`), ducked under the call. FluidSynth was not on the machine, and a generative music model was not stacked on top of the voice install.

No view-count promise. The Quiet Desk and the ML-KEM film in the other project were not built.
