A 32 gigabyte DDR5 kit used to sit on the shelf around eighty dollars. One example. Not every kit.

This October, the cheapest 32 gigabyte DDR5-6000 kit listed in the US was four hundred and fifty dollars. Another example. Same kind of shelf. Different year.

The chip did not get slower. A 16 gigabit DDR5 contract was about eight dollars and seventy cents in October 2025. By September 2026 it was about forty-eight. About five and a half times.

The wafer is why. High bandwidth memory, the kind that trains the models, takes about three times the wafer for the same memory. One die still goes in a PC. Three go in the server.

In February 2026, Crucial's retail shipments had ended. The hook is empty.

Your RAM did not get worse. The wafer left.
